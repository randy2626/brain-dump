Brain Dump

A little notebook for whatever's rattling around.
How it works
  • Type or tap the mic and say what’s on your mind. Just get it out.
  • The dropdown under the box is where the next item goes. Pick “No category” if you don’t want a page yet.
  • Type “add milk to Ideas” to file it on that page (it’ll make the page if it’s new). Or type a page name in the box below and press Enter.
  • Tap ✓ when you’re done. An item’s ⋯ lets you edit, set a date, move it, or delete it (you can undo a delete).
  • Drag an item onto another page to move it. On a phone, ⋯ → Move to… does the same.
  • When you talk, a pause, “next,” or a comma starts a new thought. “Move buy milk to Home” files something you’ve already added.
  • Add this to your Home Screen so your notes stay put on this device.
